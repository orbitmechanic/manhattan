Upper-floor facades and roofs
=============================

Generated by
  python3 scripts/build_upper_facade_library.py
  blender -b manhattan_floors.blend --python scripts/assign_upper_facades.py

                          balconies, occasional window AC units
    industrial   80..159  corrugated metal, concrete panels, steel-framed
                          ribbon windows, vents, rust streaks

Per-building assignment
  scripts/assign_upper_facades.py reads the first-floor wall loops in
  building_perimeters.json, matches every city face to its building, and
  bakes a 24-bit choice into COLOR_0 RGB:

    bits  0..8   facade tile index (0..511)
    bits  9..16  roof tile index (0..255)
    bits 17..19  facade tint variation (0..7)
    bits 20..23  facade U offset (0..15)
    alpha        block id / 1536 (unchanged)

  One facade tile is used for every wall face of a building, on every floor
  from the second floor to the top.  One roof tile is used for every
  horizontal face of the building.  Tint and U offset make adjacent buildings
  differ even when they share a template.

Shader
  viewer/assets/shaders/city_block.wesl decodes the packed value per fragment.
  Wall faces sample the facade atlas with U tiled around the building and V
  mapped to one floor; horizontal faces sample the roof atlas with a 16 m
  world-space tile.  Day and night atlases are blended with the existing
  block/twilight state, so windows and roofs light up with the city.
