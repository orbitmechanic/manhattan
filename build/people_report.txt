Pedestrians, homeless figures and corner advertising
==================================================

Catalog (built once at startup)
  Mannequin meshes:
    male torso, female torso, head, short hair, long hair, arm, leg,
    skirt, hat, bag, prone body
  Clothing catalog:
    viewer/assets/textures/people/clothing_atlas.jpg
    8 x 8 tiles of 128 px; shirts 0-15, pants 16-31, skirts 32-39,
    jackets 40-47, hats 48-55, bags 56-61
  Materials are created once from the atlas using StandardMaterial uv
  transforms.  Individual pedestrians choose shared handles at spawn; no
  per-person texture or mesh is retained in the catalog.

Pedestrian behaviour
  - Each walker gets a mitered track offset 1-12 ft (0.30-3.66 m) outside the
    building's first-floor perimeter.  The track follows that offset around
    the building.
  - Random start position, clockwise or counter-clockwise direction, and a
    walking speed of 0.85-1.5 m/s.
  - 50% male / 50% female; 10% juvenile at 30-80% adult scale.
  - Random outfit: shirt, pants, skirt (female only), jacket/arms, hat, bag.
  - Walk cycle: arms and legs are separate limb entities rotating around the
    shoulder/hip, with a small body bob.  The cycle frequency follows walking
    speed.
  - Avoidance: same-direction walkers slow/stop within 1.6 m of the walker
    ahead; oncoming walkers shift laterally by 0.35 m to pass each other.
  - Density: 1 person per 5 ft of perimeter nominally (0.2/ft), rising to
    1 person per 1 ft at the morning and evening rush-hour peaks (Gaussian
    around 08:00 and 17:30).

Homeless figures
  - One per 20 ft of building perimeter, in addition to walkers.
  - Low-poly prone body, head and hair lying against the building perimeter,
    offset 1-3 ft outward.  They do not move.

Corner advertising
  - 12% of the sign population is generated as a vertical banner running up a
    randomly chosen building corner (3-16 m tall, 1-1.9 m wide).
  - Of the remaining signs, 50% are perpendicular blade signs and 38% are
    flat wall signs (the regular 50/50 split is rotated by the corner share).

Streaming
  - Only buildings within 140 m (native) / 100 m (web) of the camera are
    populated.  Each building's walkers/homeless are despawned when it leaves
    that radius, and the catalogue remains in memory.
  - Global visible limits: 1,000 walkers / 300 homeless native;
    500 / 150 web.  Per-building limits: 100 walkers / 20 homeless.
  - No crowd or accessory data is retained for distant buildings.
