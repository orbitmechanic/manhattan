Street centreline stationing
============================

Generated by
  /tmp/opencode/geoenv/bin/python scripts/build_station_lines.py

Inputs
  build/ground_cells.npz                Voronoi ground tiles (mid-street boundaries)
  build/blocks_labels.npy               building-block labels (footprint distance)
  build/blocks.json                     building/ground bounds
  Textures/day/ground/satellite_manhattan_ground.jpg (1792x1024 hints)

Method
  - Keep Voronoi edges shared by two different blocks.
  - Sample 7 points on each edge in the satellite; keep edges with >=4 grey,
    low-saturation road samples (rejects water/green/open land).
  - Reject edges whose midpoint is farther than 35 m from a building footprint.
  - Trace through degree-2 nodes, then join collinear chain ends within 12 m
    whose directions are opposite within ~35 degrees.
  - Simplify with RDP (0.8 m) and derive plan curves from turning angles:
    circular radius from the three-point circumcircle, left/right direction,
    and reverse-curve flag when the direction flips.
  - Export flat vertical tangent profiles (the source ground is flat); the
    format already carries vertical tangent points, grades and vertical curves
    for future profiles.

Data (build/station_lines.json, also viewer/assets/models/station_lines.json)

    id (STG_0001...), points [[x,z],...], length_m/length_ft
    station_start_ft, station_end_ft
    ticks_ft, tick_points [[x,z],...]       every 100 ft plus the end
    plan_curves[] { start/end station, radius_ft, turn_deg, direction, reverse }
    plan_tangents[] { start/end station }
    vertical { tangent_points_ft, grades, curves, start/end_elevation_ft }

Viewer
  src/stationing.rs loads the data, draws centreline/ticks with gizmos and
  builds a camera civil-coordinate array against every line:
    station_ft, offset_ft (left negative / right positive), elevation_ft
    (camera elevation minus line elevation at the nearest station),
    plan_distance_ft, nearest vertical tangent station/elevation.
  Controls: L lines, T 100-ft ticks, C civil-coordinate panel; the same
  toggles are buttons in the STATIONING / CIVIL COORDINATES panel.
  Web: ?lines=1&ticks=1 (and ?view=street) works in the wasm build.
