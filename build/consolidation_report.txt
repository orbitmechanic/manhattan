===================================

  manhattan_original.fbx     8,335,244 bytes

Conclusion: the original .blend and .fbx describe the same model.  They have
identical object names, vertex/poly counts, material names, material->texture
assignments and world-space bounds.  The .fbx was therefore treated as an
export of the .blend, and a fresh .fbx was exported from the cleaned .blend.
