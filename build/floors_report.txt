Human-scale building floors + full-geometry viewer
==================================================

Floor scale
  floor height:          3.0 world units
  facade U module:       12.0 units (one floor-texture module)
  roof UV tile:          8.0 units
  one-floor textures:    10 day + 10 night
                         Textures/day/floors/<facade>_floor.jpg
                         Textures/night/floors/<facade>_floor_night.jpg

  Each facade texture was cropped to one vertical window-row period.  Walls
  map V=0..1 inside every 3-unit floor band and repeat U around the wall;
  roofs use world-space UVs at an 8-unit tile.  A FACE attribute `floor`
  records the floor index in the art master.

Floor master (manhattan_floors.blend)
  Floor_Blocks/           1223 objects, one per city block
  faces:                  739,616
  Ground_Blocks           Voronoi ground / block ids
  Parks/                  19 park empties
  hidden source           original block master geometry

  Floor bands are separate horizontal shells inside each block object, so a
  block can be edited without splitting 100k objects.  Use the helper
  `scripts/separate_block_floors.py` on the active Block_XXXX object to turn
  its floor bands into individual floor objects when detailed editing is
  needed.

Viewer assets (viewer/assets/models)
  manhattan_city.glb      128 MB, 1223 nodes, 11 materials, 1,528,337 tris
  manhattan_ground.glb    1.4 MB, one mesh, 13,319 tris
  collision_grid.bin      4.39 MB camera-height grid
  blocks.json             block metadata

  IMPORTANT: there are no merged-region, full-facade or box-proxy LOD meshes.
  The viewer renders these real per-block floor meshes for the whole city and
  only culls them at the fog distance.  Geometry is never modified for
  rendering speed.

Per-floor day/night rates
  The shader hashes (block id, floor index = world_y/3, penumbra salt) into a
  per-floor threshold in 0.12..0.88 and width in 0.03..0.15, then
  blend = block_blend * smoothstep(threshold-width, threshold+width, progress).
  Floors therefore turn on/off at individually randomised times while their
  block is transitioning; the salt re-randomises them at each penumbra.  World
  Y is used instead of vertex colours because vertex attributes interpolate
  across the facade.  The ground material disables the variation.

Fog culling
  VisibilityRange start 0; end = clamp(fog_visibility*1.05, 2.5..24 km), with
  a 600-unit dithered fade.  Beyond that the fog is effectively opaque, so the
  real meshes are culled.  The ground is always visible.  No proxy geometry is
  substituted.

Atmosphere and focus
  DistanceFog: exponential-squared; visibility =
      clamp((6000 + camera_y*3.0) * (1 - 0.25*daylight) * weather,
            2500 .. 24000)
  weather is a slow 0.78..1.10 wave.  18 horizontal mist sheets drift over the
  city, tinted to the sky, and fade with the haze.
  Gaussian DepthOfField: sensor 2.5 m (cinematic city-scale lens),
      focus = clamp(camera_y*1.35 + 70, 90 .. 3200)
      aperture f/1.0 at street level .. f/3.6 at altitude
      max CoC 18 px .. 9 px, max_depth = fog visibility
  so blur scales with distance and saturates at the fog cutoff.

Camera collision
  collision_grid.bin:  1685 x 1304 cells, 4 m, 528,170 solid cells
  max height:          410 m
  Every frame movement is swept in <= cell/2 steps.  A wall impact removes the
  normal component and slides on the tangent; descending over a roof rests on
  it; a final push-out guarantees the camera is never left inside a building.
  The grid stores vertical columns, not bridges/overhangs.
