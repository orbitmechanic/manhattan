Lighting, street furniture and vehicle night visibility
=======================================================

Building shadows
  Removed.  The sun DirectionalLight has shadow_maps_enabled = false and the
  cascade configuration is gone.  The NotShadowCaster markers from the shadow
  experiment remain inert; they have no rendering cost when shadow maps are
  disabled.

Streetlamps
  viewer/src/street_furniture.rs streams lamps around buildings within
  330 m native / 240 m web.
  - Placement: every 24 m along each building's first-floor perimeter,
    offset 2.8 m outward onto the sidewalk.
  - Geometry: metal pole, 6.25 m arm and lamp head, plus a shared emissive
    lamp panel.
  - Lamp heads turn on at night with the city and are off during the day.
  - Lamps for a building are despawned when that building leaves the radius.

Traffic signals
  scripts/build_roadway.py writes viewer/assets/models/intersections.json
  (5,908 intersections, with position, yaw and road half-width).
  - Two signal poles are placed per nearby intersection, on opposite corners.
  - Geometry: metal pole/head with red, amber and green lens meshes.
  - One shared 14 s cycle: red 6 s, green 5 s, amber 3 s.  The active lens is
    bright by day and emissive at night; inactive lenses are dark.
  - Signals are streamed/despawned with the same 330 m / 240 m near radius.

Vehicle night visibility
  viewer/assets/textures/vehicles/vehicle_night_atlas.png (4 x 4 tiles):
  warm cabin windows, white headlights, red taillights, amber side markers and
  red/blue emergency bars.  Vehicle light meshes use per-face atlas UVs and
  turn their shared emissive material on at night.  Bodies get a subtle
  blue-grey emissive lift so silhouettes stay readable.  Far proxies carry a
  roof light too.
