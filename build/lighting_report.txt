Dynamic shadows and vehicle night lights
========================================

Building shadows
  - The sun DirectionalLight now has shadow_maps_enabled = true.
  - CascadeShadowConfigBuilder: first cascade far bound 60 m, maximum
    distance 900 m, four default cascades.  Shadows follow the real sun
    direction every frame and cover the near roadway/building field.
  - The moon light keeps shadows disabled.
  - Fog banks, sun/moon discs and stars are marked NotShadowCaster.  Without
    this the huge translucent mist sheets and sky spheres blocked the
    directional light and made the whole city render as if in shadow.
  - Signs, antennas, trees, storefront overlays, pedestrians, vehicles and
    traffic proxies are NotShadowCaster too.  The shadow pass therefore
    focuses on the real building meshes, which cast onto each other and the
    ground/sidewalk/road receivers.

Vehicle night visibility
  - New vehicle_night_atlas.png (4 x 4 tiles, 512 px) contains:
      warm cabin windows, white headlights, red taillights, amber side
      markers, red/blue emergency bars and variants.
  - The vehicle light mesh now has per-face atlas UVs and includes:
      two headlights, two taillights, side cabin windows, four amber markers,
      a roof cabin light, a rear safety marker and an emergency roof bar.
  - One shared emissive material uses the atlas; its emissive color is black
    by day and bright white at night, so the atlas colors (warm/red/amber)
    show along with the city dusk transition.
  - The vehicle body material gets a subtle blue-grey emissive lift at night
    so the car silhouette remains readable.  Both body and light materials
    are double-sided solids.
  - Far traffic proxies carry a glowing roof light as well, so distant lanes
    remain visible at night.
