Roadway pavement + sidewalks
============================

Generated by
  /tmp/opencode/roadenv/bin/python scripts/build_roadway.py

Inputs
  viewer/assets/models/station_lines_completed.json   4447 street baselines
  build/building_mask.png                             2 m building footprints
  build/blocks.json                                   world bounds

Geometry
  lane width:            12 ft (3.6576 m)
  sidewalk width:        12 ft (3.6576 m)
  lane count:            1..4, inferred from clear corridor
  curb height:           0.14 m
  asphalt elevation:     0.05 m above source ground
  sidewalk top:          asphalt + 0.14 m
  baselines with roads:  4373 of 4447 (74 too constrained/inside footprint)
  asphalt triangles:     29,434
  sidewalk triangles:    117,736
  output:                viewer/assets/models/roadway_chunks/*.glb
                         129 tiles of 500 m, 11.6 MB total
  manifest:              viewer/assets/models/roadway_manifest.json
                         (tile path + world AABB, used by DetailCull)

Method
  - For every completed centreline vertex, cast left/right normals against the
    building mask and measure clear distance.
  - lanes = floor(min_clearance / 12 ft), clamped 1..4.
  - road half-width = lanes * 12 ft / 2.
  - sidewalks = min(12 ft, remaining clearance on each side).
  - Emit asphalt quads, sidewalk top quads and vertical inner curb faces.
  - Narrow corridors shrink the road/sidewalk rather than crossing a building.

Materials
  Road_Asphalt:      dark grey (0.10, 0.10, 0.11), roughness 0.92
  Sidewalk_Concrete: light grey (0.78, 0.78, 0.75), roughness 0.86
  Both are unlit at grazing city scale, with base colors scaled by daylight in
  update_road_colors so they darken at night.

Viewer
  Each 500 m tile is spawned as its own scene root with a DetailCull AABB.
  The whole tile is hidden once the camera is more than 1000 m from the box,
  so pavement never renders outside the embellishment radius.
  --view close starts at (1010, 20, -462) looking down CMP_1713.
  Screenshot: renders/viewer_roadway_close.png

Notes
  - The geometry is derived from the default COMPLETED centreline set.
  - Lane counts are an estimate, not lane-level survey data.
  - Sidewalk widths shrink where building clearance is tight.
  - The source ground is flat; roadway follows the completed baseline elevation.
