Centreline cleanup
  - completed network re-vectorised at 1.0 m RDP tolerance (was 2.0 m),
  - two smoothing passes: 3-point weighted average, sharp corners (<25 deg
    turn) preserved, then 0.15 m RDP,
  - any smoothed segment that enters a building is rejected and the original
    polyline retained,
  - final connectors are routed through the free-space cost field.

Method
  - Split each baseline into free runs at building footprints instead of
    discarding the whole line; keep runs down to 4 m.
  - Choose one width per run: reserve sidewalk space first
    (min(12 ft, max(1.5 m, 15% of corridor))), then spend the rest on whole
    12-ft lanes.  This prevents a 20 m corridor from becoming four lanes plus
    two full sidewalks.
  - Build continuous cross-sections with mitred joins, so bends have no
    cracks or width waves.  A miter scale is clamped to 2.5 to avoid spikes.
  - Emit asphalt, sidewalk tops and vertical curb faces from the same
    cross-sections.
  - Fill every crossing/T-junction with an asphalt disk sized to the wider
    road, so intersections are continuous and do not show star-shaped gaps.
  - Paint solid white edge lines on multi-lane streets and dashed centre
    lines on 2+ lane streets.  Markings sit 4.5 mm above the asphalt and are
    chunked with the road.

Materials
  Road_Asphalt:        dark grey (0.10, 0.10, 0.11), unlit, roughness 0.92
  Sidewalk_Concrete:   light grey (0.78, 0.78, 0.75), unlit, roughness 0.86
  Road_Marking_White:  warm white (0.92, 0.92, 0.88), unlit
  All three are tinted by daylight in update_road_colors.

Viewer
  Road, sidewalk and marking tiles are spawned as scene roots with
  DetailCull AABBs and hidden beyond 1,000 m.
  --view close starts at (1010, 20, -462) looking down CMP_1713.

Verification

Notes
  - The network is a planning aid, not survey data.
  - Lane counts and sidewalk widths are estimates from the building mask.
  - A few very short or fully building-blocked runs are omitted.
  - The source ground is flat; roadway follows the completed baseline
    elevation (grade 0).
