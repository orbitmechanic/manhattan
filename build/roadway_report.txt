Roadway pavement, sidewalks, junctions and markings
===================================================

Generated by
  /tmp/opencode/osmvenv/bin/python scripts/build_street_network.py \
      /tmp/opencode/osm_highways.json viewer/assets/models/street_network.json
  python3 scripts/build_street_centrelines.py
  /tmp/opencode/roadenv/bin/python scripts/build_roadway.py

Inputs
  viewer/assets/models/street_network.json

    (4,231 named), converted to the BlenderGIS EPSG:3857 frame.

Centreline source
  - Real OSM street geometry, not the old building-front/Voronoi network.
  - Ways merged only when they share an endpoint, have the same name (or
    are both unnamed), the same oneway direction and a turn under 70 deg.
  - oneway=-1 ways are reversed, so every one-way chain points in the
    permitted travel direction.
  - Bridges and tunnels are skipped: the city mesh has no deck geometry.
  - Chains are clipped to the model ground rectangle so no pavement floats.
  - No smoothing pass is applied; the OSM polyline is already clean.

Method
  - Split each real street chain at building footprints.
  - Measure the building clearance on each side of every centreline vertex.
  - Build continuous cross-sections with mitred joins; the asphalt/sidewalk
    corridor runs to the building faces, capped at 15 m half-width.
  - The outer 12 ft of each side is sidewalk aligned to the building face;
    where the scan hits the cap the sidewalk is omitted and the junction
    remains open asphalt.
  - One-way roads emit traffic lanes only in the OSM direction.  Two-way
    roads reverse the left half so both directions are populated.
  - Pedestrian ways are paved but emit no traffic lanes or signals.
  - Fill crossings/T-junctions with an asphalt disk sized to the wider road.
  - Mark: solid white edge lines, white dashed lane dividers and a double
    yellow centreline on two-way roads only.

Materials
  Road_Asphalt:        dark grey (0.10, 0.10, 0.11), unlit, roughness 0.92
  Sidewalk_Concrete:   light grey (0.78, 0.78, 0.75), unlit, roughness 0.86
  Road_Marking_White:  warm white (0.93, 0.93, 0.89), unlit
  Road_Marking_Yellow: traffic yellow (0.95, 0.72, 0.08), unlit
  All are tinted by daylight in update_road_colors.

Viewer
  Road, sidewalk and marking tiles are spawned as scene roots with
  DetailCull AABBs and hidden beyond 1,000 m.  Traffic lanes stream vehicles
  with the same 1 km radius; no runtime geometry generation was added.
  --view close starts at (1010, 20, -462) looking down CMP_1713.

Verification
  - OSM lanes plotted over the satellite follow the real street grid.
  - The generated asphalt follows the same lanes; 0 runs were blocked by
    buildings.
  - The OSM stationing set reports 1,814 lines / 268.1 miles at runtime.
