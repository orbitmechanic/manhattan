Runtime dressing (viewer/src/storefront.rs)
  - Executed once at Startup, before the first update.
  - Each building gets a seed derived from the run seed and its block id.
  - The wall loop is split into panels roughly one image wide (8/12/6/10 m by
    category).  Panel width grows only if a huge loop would exceed the deck.
  - Panels draw without replacement from a shuffled 150-image deck, so no
    image repeats around a building's perimeter (0 repeat warnings across
    11,032 buildings).
  - Stored per panel: image_id, offset_m, width_m, scale.
  - The assignment is immutable for the run; a new run draws a new seed
    (SystemTime on native, js_sys::Math::random on wasm).
  - Quads are 0.08 m proud of the wall, 0.06 m to 2.4-3.6 m high, unlit and
    double-sided with ClampToEdge atlas sampling.
  - Every building mesh carries a DetailCull AABB and is rendered only when
    the camera is within 1000 m.
