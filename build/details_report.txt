Street detail, dawn timing, and night windows
=============================================

Dawn texture switch
  The old behaviour began switching night->day as soon as the sun entered the
  +1..-6 degree penumbra.  Dawn now holds the night set for the whole
  penumbra and only begins the shut-off after the sun reaches +1 degree.
  The switch is spread over the next 1.5 degrees (roughly a few minutes), so
  the per-block and per-floor thresholds still stagger the change instead of
  snapping simultaneously.  Dusk keeps the original in-penumbra fade.
  Verified from the viewer HUD:
    04:30  sun  0.0 deg   lit 1242/1242  (night held inside the penumbra)
    05:00  sun +4.9 deg   lit    0/1242  (after the post-penumbra switch)

Night windows
  Three facade night atlases are generated:
    facade_atlas_night.jpg        random lit subset A
    facade_atlas_night_b.jpg      independent random subset B
    facade_atlas_night_all.jpg    every window lit
  Each floor selects A or B from a per-floor hash, so the lit-window pattern
  changes floor to floor instead of repeating the same strip.  Lit
  probabilities are 28% residential, 20% industrial, 30% commercial and 42%
  financial.  Tall financial buildings (height bucket >= 2 and the building
  marked financial) give each floor a 35% chance of using the all-lit atlas.

Antennas
  Density = one antenna per floor per 10,000 sq ft of floor area:
    count = round(footprint_m2 * 10.7639 * floors / 10,000)
  with a minimum of 1 and a safety cap of 64 per building.  Current build:
  63,720 antennas on 10,815 buildings.  Heights are randomised between 1%
  and 8% of the building's total height.  Each mast is rooted using the
  baked collision grid's roof height at its random plan position, so antennas
  on setbacks and lower wings sit on the roof instead of floating at the
  building's maximum height.  Culled beyond 1,000 ft (304.8 m).

Signs
  Population is exactly four times the antenna count (current build: 255,640
  signs on 10,998 buildings).  Signs appear on every building, not just
  commercial/industrial neighbourhoods.  Half are flat wall signs parallel to
  the facade; half are blade signs mounted perpendicular to the facade and
  projecting 2.4-4.0 m.  Boards are 0.9-2.6 m tall at the second floor.
  The 64-design atlas has abstract blocky glyphs in random colours.  About
  25% blink (six phases), 25% are steadily illuminated and the rest are
  unlit.  Culled beyond 1,000 ft.

Trees
  3,000 low-poly trees in the 19 park/open-space blocks, at least 9 m from
  any street centreline and outside building footprints.  Three variants
  (round, conifer, shrub), random scale and rotation.  Culled beyond
  1,000 ft.

Viewer detail cull
  DetailCull now carries a radius.  Roads and ground-floor/upper facades keep
  the 1,000 m embellishment radius; antennas, signs and trees use 304.8 m.
