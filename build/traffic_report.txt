Vehicle traffic
===============

Catalog
  Generated once at startup in viewer/src/traffic.rs.
  150 low-poly variants: 30 each of
    tractor-trailer  10% spawn share
    cargo van        20%
    sport utility    40%
    passenger sedan  20%
    emergency        10% (ambulance / fire truck / police, 10 variants each)
  Civilian body colours are muted greys, blues, greens, browns and beiges;
  dimensions and cabin proportions vary per variant.  Emergency vehicles use
  white/red, fire-red and police dark/white schemes with roof light bars.
  One shared white body material (vertex colours) and one shared emissive
  lights material.  Both are double-sided so every vehicle is a closed solid
  from every viewing angle, not a facade.

Lane network
  Exported by scripts/build_roadway.py to
    viewer/assets/models/traffic_lanes.json
  24,175 lane centrelines, 3.0 MB.
  Left-half lanes are reversed so every vehicle travels along its lane
  centreline.  Each lane carries its width and length.

Streaming
  - Traffic now covers the same 1,000 m radius as the roadway.
  - Within 250 m native / 180 m web, lanes are populated with individually
    animated vehicles (limits 1,200 / 600).
  - Beyond the near radius, each lane is filled with a merged far-proxy mesh
    of simple solid vehicle boxes to 85% occupancy, so every visible road is
    covered out to 1 km.  Proxies are despawned when the lane leaves range.
  - Only the active near-lane, far-proxy and vehicle sets are kept.
  - Each near lane is filled to 85% occupancy (vehicle lengths plus 0.7 m
    gaps).  Vehicles are rejected if their width plus 0.35 m does not fit the
    lane, so they stay off the 12-ft sidewalk tracks.

Movement / gridlock
  - Vehicles are always aligned to the lane tangent with only +/-0.03 rad of
    yaw jitter, so they are parallel to the road and perpendicular to the
    adjacent building faces.
  - Same-lane car-following keeps at least 1.2 m clear and only allows small
    creep moves (<= 0.18 m) when space opens; a per-vehicle stop/go oscillator
    keeps the flow uneven.
  - A 6 m spatial grid of vehicle AABBs blocks cross-lane moves at
    intersections, so vehicles never overlap.
  - The normal state is dense gridlock.  Far proxies are static merged solid
    boxes, so the distant road remains filled without per-vehicle updates.

Lights
  Headlight boxes and side cabin-light strips use one shared emissive
  material.  It is black during the day and warm white at night, following the
  Environment day/night state with the rest of the city.
