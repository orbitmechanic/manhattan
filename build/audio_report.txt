Spatial city audio
==================

Assets
  viewer/assets/sounds/*.ogg are synthesized by scripts/generate_audio.py
  (numpy + ffmpeg/libvorbis), avoiding sampled/licensed material.

    ambient_city.ogg  14 s stereo loop: low city rumble, air, 55/110 Hz hum
    wind.ogg           9 s stereo loop: filtered rush with slow gusts
    traffic.ogg        8 s stereo loop: tyre/engine rumble
    rain.ogg           9 s stereo loop: hiss, body, random droplet ticks
    thunder.ogg        7 s one-shot: low rumble plus several cracks
    horn1/2/3.ogg      short synthesized two-tone horns

Spatial design
  Bevy's audio backend provides stereo panning (not HRTF), so:
    - ambient city, wind and rain are non-spatial stereo beds around the
      listener;
    - four traffic loops are spatial emitters repositioned every 0.15 s to the
      four nearest animated vehicles; volume falls off with distance;
    - distant horns spawn every 4-20 s at random 90-610 m positions;
    - thunder spawns at the same world position as the visible lightning bolt,
      delayed by 0.35 s + distance / 343 m/s;
    - distance attenuation is applied explicitly via Volume before playback.

Mix
  ambient city  0.16
  wind          0.02 .. 0.32, scaled by actual camera speed
  rain          0 .. 0.30, scaled by precipitation (softer in snow)
  traffic       0 .. 0.20, nearest four vehicles within 170 m
  horns         0.02 .. 0.11
  thunder       0.08 .. 0.48

  Volumes are smoothed with exponential approach to avoid clicks.  Traffic
  audio fades out while high-speed accessory suspension is active.

Web policy / quit
  Browsers block autoplay.  The web build starts loop sinks paused and the
  AudioGate unlocks on the first key press or left click.  Native starts
  immediately.  All one-shot sounds are gated until audio is enabled.
  AppExit pauses all native sinks; a pagehide/beforeunload handler closes the
  browser AudioContexts so ambience does not continue after the tab/window
  closes.

Files
  viewer/src/audio.rs          handles, levels, traffic/horn/thunder logic
  scripts/generate_audio.py    asset synthesis
  viewer/tools/build_web.sh    stages assets/sounds into web/dist
